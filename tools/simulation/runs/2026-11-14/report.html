HVAC offline workbench

Objective: reduce overshoot-driven hysteresis shutdowns, preserve effective recovery, and assess temperature deviations by magnitude and duration.

Conditional response replay: recorded future commands, enablement, weather and fireplace state are supplied inputs. This is not an operational forecast with only information known at the origin. Persistence/trend baselines have less input information. Gain-search counterfactuals generate their own commands and enablement.

Qualification is a research screening gate. It does not establish that alternative gains improve the house. Predictions run without future temperature, fusion-weight or own operating-state feedback. All three heads' simultaneous counterfactual interaction is outside this version's scope.
